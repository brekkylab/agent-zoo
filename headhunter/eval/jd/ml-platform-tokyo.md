# ML Platform Engineer — Tokyo

## The team

Sundial. We run the training and serving infrastructure that four modeling teams
share. The platform started as one person's scheduler scripts and is now load-bearing
for the whole company, which is the problem — it works, and nobody wants to touch it.

## What you would do

- Own GPU scheduling on Kubernetes. Jobs currently queue behind each other in ways
  the submitting team cannot see or predict
- Rebuild the training orchestration layer for PyTorch jobs so a run is reproducible
  from its inputs rather than from whoever remembers how it was launched
- Take over the feature store the modeling teams read from

## Must have

- **Kubernetes, MLOps, and PyTorch.** Not one of the three — the job sits where they
  meet
- **Based in Tokyo.** The office is here and the hardware is in the room
- Ownership of a platform other teams depended on

## Nice to have

- GPU scheduling or capacity planning at scale
- Feature store or data platform work
- Experience taking over a system somebody else built

## How we hire

We send InMail to a shortlist. Tell us who you would not contact and why — the
rejections are as useful to us as the picks.
