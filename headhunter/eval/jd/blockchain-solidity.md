# Smart Contract Engineer (Solidity)

## The team

Ridgeline. We are building settlement rails on-chain and need someone who has shipped
contracts that held real value.

## What you would do

- Write and audit Solidity contracts for a settlement protocol
- Own the upgrade path — proxy patterns, storage layout, migration safety
- Work with the security firm through audit cycles

## Must have

- **Solidity in production.** Contracts you wrote that held funds, not tutorials
- Familiarity with common vulnerability classes and how they are found
- 3+ years of engineering experience

## Nice to have

- Formal verification or fuzzing experience
- EVM internals
- Prior audit engagement, on either side

## How we hire

We send InMail to a shortlist.

If nobody in the pool qualifies, say so. A shortlist of people who are close but do
not meet the bar costs us more than an empty one — we would rather widen the search
than interview the wrong five.
